7 · Sensitivity and estimator warnings

  • Hill tail indices depend on the tail fraction k (recorded ~0.9 IQR swing across 2.5–10%); the k region is marked on the CCDF figure.
  • ACF uncertainty bands assume iid data and understate uncertainty under volatility clustering.
  • Aggregation-profile points at large horizons rest on few aggregated observations; effective n is printed on the figure.
  • Scalar metrics can miss anomalies a figure shows — and a figure can suggest structure a formal test would not support. Neither substitutes for the other.
